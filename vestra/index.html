About

Vestra is a UK-focused, privacy-first net-worth tracker. Bank accounts and debt, Premium Bonds, shares and ETFs, crypto, metals, property and collectables sit in one live-priced view, and the arithmetic is done properly: returns that ignore deposits, an allocation X-ray that splits ETFs into their real sector weights, target weights that show drift and the trades that rebalance, and a fee X-ray that shows twenty years of fund charges.

Three principles hold it together. Private by design: prices come from keyless public feeds, wallets are read on-chain and read-only, and a privacy blur hides every figure. Honest numbers: time-weighted and money-weighted returns, FX attribution and a benchmark overlay, so a gain is only ever a gain. Everything counts: if you own it, it belongs in the total.
