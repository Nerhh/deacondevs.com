      About

      I'm Marcus Deacon — 30, UK born and raised, and a father. By day I build software: browser tooling, data-heavy apps, and small sharp utilities that quietly save people money. The current obsession is Umbra, a browser where every tab is its own isolated machine — alongside ticket-price analysis and personal wealth tracking.

      Off the clock it's LEGO — proper sets and freeform builds — and Old School RuneScape, which taught me everything I know about long-term incremental progress. Hence the interface. This site is hand-built: HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript doing every animation you can see, including the fires and the duel up top.
