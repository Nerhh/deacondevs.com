umbra

beta · active

A multi-session desktop browser where every tab is its own isolated browser — its own cookies and storage, its own proxy and exit IP, its own coherent fingerprint. Built for people who run many accounts, test across regions, or need to see what a page actually looks like from somewhere else.

  • Sealed sessions — cookies, storage and cache never cross between tabs.
  • One exit per tab — bring your own proxies; Umbra tests, assigns and rotates them.
  • A coherent device — screen, GPU, fonts and clock set together, tracking the proxy.
  • electron
  • react
  • typescript
  • go
  • next.js
